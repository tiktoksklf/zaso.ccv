Subscription, Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective October 17, 2026

Monthly subscription billing

Pro costs $4.99 USD per month and Elite costs $9.99 USD per month, unless the price at checkout reflects a promotion or other adjustment. Applicable taxes, if any, are shown during checkout. Paid subscriptions renew automatically until canceled. Stripe securely processes payments.

How to cancel

Sign in to your zaso.cc dashboard, select Manage billing / Cancel, and follow the cancellation instructions in the Stripe Customer Portal. You can cancel without contacting support. Make sure you complete the cancellation and review the confirmation shown by Stripe.

Your billing portal confirmation shows whether cancellation takes effect immediately or at the end of the current billing period. If cancellation is scheduled for period end, paid access continues until that date and the subscription will not renew afterward. Canceling does not automatically reverse charges already processed.

Subscription charges are generally nonrefundable except where required by law or where we agree to make a correction for a billing error. If you believe you were charged incorrectly, contact the verified support address below with the date and charge amount. We will review requests individually. Nothing in this policy limits statutory consumer rights.

Plan upgrades or downgrades may affect the next charge or create prorated adjustments, as shown in the Stripe billing portal. If a renewal payment fails, paid access may be interrupted or downgraded, subject to the payment-retry and grace-period rules then in effect.
